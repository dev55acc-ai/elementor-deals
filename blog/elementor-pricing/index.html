How Much Does Elementor Actually Cost in 2026?

Updated September 2026 · All prices verified against Elementor's site

The one-line answer

Elementor Pro starts at $60/yr for 1 site on today's first-year pricing (list $99/yr), and the flagship Elementor One — the all-in-one plan with Editor Pro + AI + image optimization + accessibility + email — runs $180/yr first year (renews at $228/yr). Free is $0 forever but missing the headline Pro features.

Elementor Pro price table (first-year, verified today)

  • Essential — 1 site — $99/yr renewal, $60/yr first year.
  • Advanced Solo — 1 site, all 85 widgets — $84/yr first year.
  • Advanced — 3 sites — $108/yr first year.
  • Expert — 25 sites — $204/yr first year.
  • Elementor One — 1 site, everything bundled — $180/yr first year (renews $228/yr).
  • One Agency — unlimited sites — $432/yr first year (renews $540/yr).

Paying monthly (Essential, $9.99/mo) is always worse than annual — annual is effectively 2 months free, and the sale multiplies that.

The Elementor Cloud Website

Elementor's managed hosting bundles: Cloud Website (single site, hosting, CDN, SSL, and Pro) typically $119/yr on sale (list $179). For one site it's often the cheapest path to a fully-Pro website. The Agency Cloud plan bundles client-hosting access and is aimed at freelancers billing out sites $1k+.

Add-ons that quietly add cost

  • Elementor AI (Write/Code/Image features) — one-time credit packs or subscription; skip it unless you practice with it daily.
  • Third-party widgets (Exclusive, Dynamic.ooo, etc.) — $30–$70/yr each; every “adds 50 widgets” plugin is a recurring line item.
  • Hosting for your site — Hostinger Premium ($23.88/yr on promo) is a sensible pairing for a single site; we covered that here.

So the cheapest legit setup

  1. Buy while Elementor's first-year promo pricing is live (it is right now).
  2. Buy Essential at $60/yr if you run one site and won't use AI; One at $180/yr if you want the full Suite.
  3. Host on Hostinger Premium ($23.88/yr), not cloud-priced hosting.
  4. Total: ~$84–204 for a full year — less than a designer's hourly rate.
