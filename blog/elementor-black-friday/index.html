The short answer

Elementor consistently runs its biggest discount of the year during Black Friday / Cyber Monday — in recent years that has meant 40% off all Elementor Pro plans, bringing Essentials from $99 down to $60/year. There is no separate coupon code: the discount applies site-wide at checkout during the sale window.

When the sale starts

Elementor's BFCM window has reliably opened the week before Thanksgiving(around Nov 20–25) and runs through Cyber Monday, sometimes extended a few days. Our advice: don't wait for the exact date — from mid-November, check this page weekly. We update it the moment the sale goes live.

What to expect on pricing

  • Essentials (1 site): ~$60/yr during the sale (regularly $99).
  • Advanced (3 sites): ~$95–99/yr (regularly $199).
  • Expert (25 sites): ~$120–145/yr (regularly $299).
  • Historically the discount is 40% flat across all tiers — the higher tiers are where the dollar savings get big.

Should you wait, or buy now?

  • Waiting saves ~$40/year if your renewal can line up with a sale window — Elementor runs smaller 30–40% sales several times a year.
  • Buying now is fine if you have a project due: you're not locked out of future sales, and Elementor's renewals often match the discounted launch price.
  • Also compare the bundle: Elementor Cloud Website (hosting + Pro in one) often undercuts buying hosting and Pro separately — and its own deals stack during BFCM. See our Cloud Website review.

FAQ

How much does Elementor Pro cost on Black Friday?
In recent years, 40% off across all Pro plans. Essentials drops to about $60/yr, Advanced to about $95–99/yr. The 2026 numbers will be confirmed here on the day.
Is there a Black Friday coupon code?
No code needed — the discount is applied automatically at checkout during the sale window. Any site asking for a 'code' is chasing traffic.
Do Black Friday prices apply to renewals?
The sale price applies to new purchases and upgrades. For renewals, the reliable move is grabbing the sale window right before your renewal date.
Does Elementor Cloud hosting go on sale too?
Yes — the Cloud Website bundle has been included in past BFCM promotions, which stacks hosting savings on top of Pro savings.
